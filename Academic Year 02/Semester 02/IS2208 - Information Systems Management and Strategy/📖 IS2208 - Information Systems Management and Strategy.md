#Lesson-Plan 

> [!important] Goal
> The primary goal of **IS2208: Information Systems Management and Strategy** is to equip students with the theoretical frameworks and practical acumen required to leverage information systems for strategic competitive advantage. The curriculum explores strategic alignment between business and IT, strategic frameworks, IT governance, digital transformation, and strategic resource allocation in enterprise environments.

---

# The specific objectives of the module include:

- **Strategic Alignment:** Formulating and evaluating alignment models between business strategy and information technology architecture.
- **Strategic Analytical Frameworks:** Applying strategic models such as Porter's Five Forces, Value Chain Analysis, and the Resource-Based View (RBV) to assess digital capabilities.
- **IT Governance & Risk Management:** Implementing governance frameworks (COBIT, ITIL) to oversee IT investments, risk mitigation, and regulatory compliance.
- **Enterprise Digital Transformation:** Navigating organizational change management, business model innovation, and disruptive technologies in contemporary markets.
- **IT Portfolio & Investment Planning:** Assessing the financial feasibility, ROI, and total cost of ownership (TCO) for enterprise information systems projects.

# Topic Covered

1. [[Information Transmission and Communication Technologies]]
	- **Foundations of Information Transmission:** Tactical and strategic velocity, organizational flow boundaries, and the high-availability reliability triad ($D = T \times (1 - A)$).
	- **Transmission Media Taxonomy:** Physical wired (copper, coaxial, optical fiber), wireless channels (Wi-Fi, cellular, radio), and the hybrid backbone paradigm (Amazon fulfillment centers).
	- **Next-Generation Telecom:** Wi-Fi 7 (802.11be), Private 5G enterprise networks, and LEO satellite constellations (Starlink) with data sovereignty implications.
	- **Enterprise Network Scope Hierarchy:** LAN, WAN (FedEx global private network), the public internet, and secure corporate intranets (Intel global portal).
	- **Modern Collaboration Ecosystems:** Synchronous vs. asynchronous communication architectures (Shopify digital HQ benchmark).
	- **The AI Evolution in Workplace Platforms:** Transitioning from passive text assistants to autonomous action agents (Microsoft Copilot Studio, Salesforce Slack Agentforce, Zoom AI Companion 3.0).
	- **Web 2.0 vs. Web 3.0 & Enterprise Blockchain Reality Check:** Participatory collective intelligence vs. decentralized cryptographic ledgers; case post-mortems of TradeLens and IBM Food Trust; the shared database acid test.
	- **Cloud Communications & UCaaS:** Economic agility (CapEx to OpEx, Zoom pandemic scaling) vs. critical internet dependency, lock-in, and compliance risks.
	- **Strategic Enterprise IoT & Servitization:** 4-tier cyber-physical IoT architecture, edge vs. cloud analytics, and Rolls-Royce "Power by the Hour" business model transformation.
	- **Organizational Crowdsourcing:** Crowd innovation (Dell IdeaStorm), crowd problem-solving (InnoCentive), crowd intelligence, and crowd labor.
	- **Emerging Horizons:** 5G-Advanced (Release 20), 6G standardization roadmap (2026–2029), and edge computing fabrics.

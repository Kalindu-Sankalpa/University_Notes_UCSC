#Lesson-Plan 

> [!important] Goal
> This course aims to provide students with the theoretical foundation and practical skills necessary to manage, secure, and govern data assets effectively. The course integrates advanced database management techniques with organizational data governance principles to ensure data quality, compliance, and ethical use in modern digital environments.

---

# The specific objectives of the module include:

- **Evolution of Data Management:** Explain the evolution of data management and its strategic role in driving enterprise digital transformation.
- **Advanced Database Mechanisms:** Implement advanced database mechanisms such as triggers, stored procedures, views, and indexing.
- **Transaction Management & Query Optimization:** Apply ACID transaction management and query optimization techniques for database performance improvement.
- **Secure & Distributed Databases:** Design secure and distributed database architectures that safeguard integrity and support scalable access.
- **Core Governance Concepts:** Explain core concepts of data governance, including data stewardship, ownership, data lineage, and data quality metrics.
- **Data Governance Frameworks:** Develop and evaluate data governance frameworks aligned with organizational policies and compliance standards.
- **Ethical, Legal & Privacy Considerations:** Assess ethical, privacy, and legal frameworks governing modern information systems and data handling.
- **Tools & Practices for Data Quality & Security:** Apply tools and industry practices to ensure data integrity, robust security controls, and high data quality within organizations.

---

# Topic Covered

1. [[Introduction to Data Governance and Management]]
	- **Strategic Dichotomy:** Differentiating governance authority ("Doing the right thing") from technical execution ("Doing it right").
	- **Data Structures & Career Trajectories:** Classifying structured, semi-structured, and unstructured data alongside technical, governance, and strategic career paths.
	- **Enterprise Data Lifecycle (DLM):** Tracing the 8-stage pipeline from acquisition through OLAP warehousing, Data Lakes, to certified destruction.
	- **Database System Components & DBLC:** The 5 foundational components (Hardware, Software, People, Procedures, Data) and the 6-phase Database Life Cycle.
	- **Modern Data Models & DBaaS:** Comparative evaluation of Relational, Object-Oriented, NoSQL, Vector/Time-Series frontier engines, and Cloud Database-as-a-Service architectures.

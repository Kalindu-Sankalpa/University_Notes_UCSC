> [!important] Goal
> This course moves from understanding the user (UX) to crafting the visual interface (UI), emphasizing a human-centered design process bridging theory with practical, hands-on digital product design. Students develop the foundational mindset, empirical methodologies, and industry skills (utilizing Figma as the core design tool) to create accessible, effective, and compelling digital experiences.

# The specific objectives of the module include:

- **Human-Centered Design (HCD) & Design Thinking:** Mastering the 5-stage iterative design thinking process (Empathize, Define, Ideate, Prototype, Test) aligned with ISO 9241-210 pillars.
- **Empirical User Research & Empathy:** Conducting qualitative and quantitative user research, formulating semi-structured interviews, empathy mapping, and synthesizing research into actionable user personas.
- **Usability Heuristics & Cognitive Psychology:** Critiquing interfaces using Jakob Nielsen’s 10 Usability Heuristics, cognitive walkthroughs, and key UX laws (Fitts's Law, Hick's Law, Miller's Law, Jacob's Law).
- **Information Architecture & Interaction Flows:** Structuring complex content, executing card sorting techniques, designing sitemaps, and mapping end-to-end user and task flows.
- **Component Systems & Prototyping in Figma:** Transitioning from low-fidelity wireframes to high-fidelity component-driven interactive prototypes utilizing 8pt layout grids, accessible typography scales, and tokenized styles.
- **Accessibility (A11y) & Ethical UX:** Enforcing WCAG 2.2 AA standards, keyboard navigation paths, visible focus indicators, and eliminating manipulative dark patterns (EU Digital Services Act compliance).

# Topic Covered

### Part 1: Foundations & User Empathy (Weeks 1–4)

1. [[Introduction to UI-UX & Design Thinking]]
	- **HCI vs UX vs UI:** Distinguishing conceptual boundaries, outputs, and cross-functional product team synergies.
	- **Design Thinking Methodology:** The 5 iterative, non-linear stages (Empathize, Define, Ideate, Prototype, Test) and deliverable artifacts.
	- **Human-Centered Design (HCD):** ISO 9241-210 standards, context of use, and multi-disciplinary lifecycle management.
	- **Accessibility & Ethics:** WCAG 2.2 AA non-negotiable baselines, cognitive accommodations, and anti-dark pattern compliance (EU DSA).

2. [[User Research & Personas]]
	- **Research Philosophy:** Grounding design in empirical evidence and empathy rather than designer assumptions.
	- **Methodological Taxonomy:** Qualitative vs quantitative, exploratory vs evaluative research.
	- **Primary Qualitative Methods:** Contextual inquiry, observational shadowing, diary studies, and the think-aloud protocol.
	- **Synthesis Artifacts:** Structuring 4-quadrant empathy maps (Says, Thinks, Does, Feels), behavioral user personas, and task analysis.

3. [[Usability Heuristics & UX Laws]]
	- **Expert Inspection Methods:** Cognitive Walkthrough procedure (the 4 core questions) and Heuristic Evaluation protocols.
	- **Nielsen's 10 Usability Heuristics:** In-depth breakdown with concrete violations and design solutions.
	- **Core Psychological UX Laws:** Fitts's Law, Hick's Law, Miller's Law, Jakob's Law, Aesthetic-Usability Effect, and Doherty Threshold.
	- **Norman's Conceptual Models:** Resolving the Gulf of Execution and Gulf of Evaluation.

4. [[Information Architecture & User Flows]]
	- **Organizational Systems:** Hierarchical, sequential, and hypertext matrix architectures.
	- **Content Structuring & Taxonomy:** Open and closed card sorting techniques, metadata tagging, and controlled vocabularies.
	- **Wayfinding & Navigation:** Sitemaps, global vs local navigation, breadcrumbs, search indexing, and progressive disclosure.
	- **Flow Diagramming:** Mapping user flows, decision branches, error pathways, and wireflows.

> [!important] Goal
> The primary goal of **IS2210: Applied Data Science** is to teach the complete data science workflow, turning raw structured and unstructured data into actionable predictive insights and automated decision support systems. The course emphasizes practical problem-solving using scientific programming environments, statistical inference, feature engineering, and core machine learning paradigms.

---

# The specific objectives of the module include:

- **End-to-End Data Science Pipeline:** Executing stages of data collection, wrangling, exploratory data analysis (EDA), model training, and performance evaluation.
- **Statistical Foundations & Inference:** Applying probability distributions, hypothesis testing, and statistical estimation to validate data patterns.
- **Feature Engineering & Transformation:** Preprocessing continuous and categorical variables, handling missingness, outlier detection, dimensionality reduction, and scaling.
- **Predictive Machine Learning:** Training and validating supervised algorithms (Regression, Decision Trees, Ensembles) and unsupervised models (Clustering, PCA).
- **Model Evaluation & Interpretation:** Evaluating model performance through precision-recall trade-offs, ROC-AUC, confusion matrices, and explainability techniques.

---

# Topic Covered

1. [[Introduction to Data Science]]
	- **Foundations of Data Science:** Definitions, data classifications (structured, semi-structured, unstructured), and evolution from 1960s statistics to modern AI.
	- **Analytics Hierarchy:** Descriptive ("What happened?"), diagnostic ("Why did it happen?"), predictive ("What will happen?"), and prescriptive ("What should we do?").
	- **Data Science Lifecycle & Roles:** The 6-stage lifecycle, contrasting Data Scientists vs Data Engineers vs Machine Learning Engineers.
	- **Ethics, Privacy & Bias:** Regulatory compliance (GDPR, Sri Lanka PDPA 2022), algorithmic fairness, transparency, and accountability.

2. [[Data Collection & Preparation]]
	- **Data Ingestion Methods:** Observational vs transactional data, primary vs secondary sources, open datasets, and survey sampling.
	- **Probability & Non-Probability Sampling:** Simple random, systematic, stratified, cluster, convenience, quota, and snowball sampling.
	- **Web Scraping & APIs:** HTTP scraping mechanics vs REST APIs, JSON data normalization, and social media data collection.
	- **Data Cleaning & Quality:** Handling missing data mechanisms (MCAR, MAR, MNAR), outlier mitigation, structural deduplication, and the 6 quality dimensions.
	- **Transformation & Big Data:** Feature selection, PCA dimensionality reduction, discretization/binning, the 5 V's of Big Data, compression, and chunking.

3. [[Data Visualization]]
	- **Purpose & Psychology:** Exploratory vs explanatory visualization, Anscombe's Quartet, and visual encoding accuracy (Cleveland & McGill).
	- **Chart Typology:** Selecting optimal charts for continuous, categorical, time-series, and multi-variable distributions.
	- **Design Principles & Pitfalls:** Edward Tufte's data-ink ratio, color theory (sequential, diverging, categorical), and avoiding misleading visualizations.
	- **Python Visualization Ecosystem:** Hands-on plotting with Matplotlib (OOP vs pyplot), Pandas plotting, Seaborn statistical graphics, and Plotly interactive charts.
